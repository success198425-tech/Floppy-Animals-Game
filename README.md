# 🐒 Floppy Animals — Beta 0.6
A fun, polished flappy-style adventure game with animal characters, customizable themes, and power-up features!

---

## ✨ Latest Updates — Beta 0.6
### ✅ Critical Fixes
- Fixed `GameOverView` protocol conformance error
- Resolved missing braces & incomplete code blocks
- Eliminated false collision/game over triggers
- Corrected unique sound effects for all 4 animals
- Fixed inconsistent pipe gaps in landscape mode
- Optimized performance for smoother gameplay

### 🎨 New Features
- **5 Customizable Themes**: Default, Neon Cyberpunk, Soft Pastel, **Dark Luxury Gold ⭐ RECOMMENDED**, Cartoon Adventure
- Adaptive text colors that stay readable against every theme
- **Gun Power-up System**: Green (3 bullets), Purple (5 bullets), Red (7 bullets) with auto-fire & explosion effects
- **Coin Economy**: 100 Score = 1 Coin, saved locally on your device
- **Difficulty-based High Score Tracking**: Separate best scores for Easy / Medium / Expert
- **Bypass/Invincibility Mode**: Toggle shield to skip obstacles for testing or fun
- **Milestone Notifications**: Special "Super Score" alerts with sound effects
- Realistic 3D bamboo pipes & smooth animal flying animations

---

## 🎮 Game Features
- 🐾 **4 Playable Characters**: Monkey 🐒 | Chicken 🐔 | Bird 🐦 | Eagle 🦅 — each with unique jump strength & sounds
- ⚖️ **3 Difficulty Levels**: Easy (wider gaps) | Medium | Expert (narrower gaps, faster speed)
- 📱 **Full Orientation Support**: Works perfectly in both portrait & landscape on all iPhones
- 🔊 **Complete Sound Set**: Jump, Score, Game Over, Pickup, Shoot, Explosion, Select, Milestone
- 💾 **Persistent Settings**: Selected theme, sound toggle, and high scores saved between launches

---

## 🚀 How to Play
1. Choose your favorite animal, difficulty level, and visual theme
2. Tap anywhere on screen to make your character fly upward
3. Dodge bamboo pipes — collect gun power-ups to blast obstacles away!
4. Score points, earn coins, and beat your personal best high score!
